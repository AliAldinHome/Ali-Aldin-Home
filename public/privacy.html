Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last Updated: 2025

Plain language summary: Ali Aldin collects your contact information to respond to real estate enquiries and (with your consent) to send periodic market updates and seasonal greetings. Your data is never sold. You can unsubscribe or request deletion at any time.

1. Who We Are

This privacy policy applies to Ali Aldin, REALTOR® Salesperson, operating as The Heartland Agent, registered with the Real Estate Council of Ontario (RECO) and a member of the Canadian Real Estate Association (CREA).

Contact: Ali Aldin · 480 Eglinton Ave W #30, Mississauga, ON L5R 0G2 · 416.606.0494 · aaldin.home@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this website, we may collect:

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • Property address and details (for valuation requests)
  • Date of birth (optional — used only for birthday greetings)
  • Property anniversary date (optional — used only for milestone emails)
  • Budget, timeline, and real estate preferences
  • How you heard about us (for business analytics only)
  • Notes and other information you voluntarily provide

We do not collect government ID, financial account numbers, or sensitive personal information.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry — regardless of marketing consent
  • Provide real estate services you have requested
  • Send marketing communications (market updates, greetings) — only if you have given express consent
  • Maintain our client relationship in compliance with REBBA/TRESA obligations
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations as a registered RECO agent

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Consent & Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL)

We comply with the Electronic Commerce Protection Act (CASL). Marketing emails (quarterly market updates, seasonal greetings) are sent only to contacts who have provided express consent by checking the opt-in box on our form.

Transactional communications (responses to your enquiry, valuation reports) are sent based on implied consent arising from your existing business relationship with us, as permitted under CASL s.10(9).

We maintain consent records including the date, method, and source of consent for a minimum of 3 years after the business relationship ends, as required by CASL.

5. Your Rights (PIPEDA)

Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent and unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)

To exercise any of these rights, contact Ali Aldin at aaldin.home@gmail.com or 416.606.0494.

6. Unsubscribe

Every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe link. You may also unsubscribe by:

  • Visiting this unsubscribe page
  • Emailing aaldin.home@gmail.com with "UNSUBSCRIBE" in the subject
  • Calling or texting 416.606.0494

Unsubscribe requests are processed immediately and automatically. You will not receive further marketing communications once processed. Note: unsubscribing from marketing does not prevent Ali from responding to an active real estate transaction or enquiry.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored in Airtable (Airtable Inc., USA) which maintains SOC 2 Type II compliance. Email communications are processed through Google Gmail (Google LLC, USA). Both providers maintain data processing agreements and appropriate security measures.

As a registered RECO agent, Ali Aldin is subject to REBBA/TRESA obligations regarding client confidentiality and record keeping.

8. Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as there is an active business relationship. If you withdraw consent and there is no active transaction, we will delete your marketing data within 30 days. RECO requires agents to retain transaction-related records for a minimum of 6 years.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact & Complaints
